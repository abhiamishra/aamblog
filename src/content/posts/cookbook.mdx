# Cookbook

Cookbook is the simplest way to create, share, and send recipes. It requires no login, is free to use,
and supports your cooking dreams. 

The joy of your creations deserves to be shared amongst everyone. With Cookbook, it happens under minutes.

Make your recipe and share it your friends here: www.yourcookbook.co

## Why not an app

Because not everything needs to be an app.

I want simple, ephemeral creation of recipes with the immediate call to action to share these recipes.

No hassle, no login, no setup.

## What is Cookbook not?
Let's start with what it isn't.

It isn't:
- a calorie tracking app
- an accurate calorie tracking app
- a recipe app to store your recipes in a collection (yet.. we'll see if people want this)

## So, what is Cookbook?

With Cookbook, you can:
- easily create recipes,
- add ingredients (in any language), 
- get a rough estimation of macros,
- share it with your friends.

That's literally it. 

Your friends can copy the recipe if they want to and add edits. Or, they can just download an image and save it on their phone.

### Integrations
At Cookbook, we also know that you track your calories with much more accurate platforms.

As such, we have a "Copy Meal Description" that copies it such that you can paste it other apps such as Macrofactor.

For the above meal shown (which you can access here: https://yourcookbook.co/r/Xw0uCaaD), the following is the meal description:

"75 g Onuva Vital Wheat Gluten (Seitan), 150 g White Rice, 50 g Onions and Tomato Salad"

Simply paste it into your calorie tracking app of your choice and enjoy!

We don't care about your macros, we care about your recipes :)

### Multilingualism
Make recipes with the culture you grew up with.

Write your ingredients in any language - Hindi, Spanish, Tagalog... our AI-powered application
can decipher and give you a rough summary of what you have.
